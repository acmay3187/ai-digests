AI Labs Research Digest

Week of Nov 9, 2026 (Nov 6 – Nov 13)

Here's what the major AI labs published this week.

Anthropic

The components of agent memory

Nov 6, 2026 · Engineering

Anthropic explains how Claude decides what to remember across agent sessions, and describes a "dreaming" process in which the model reorganizes its stored memories and corrects earlier mistakes between tasks. It is a practical look at how long-running agents keep useful context without piling up errors.

Testing Claude on real-world electronics design

Nov 9, 2026 · Science

From short prompts, Claude designed three electronic devices end to end. Diode Computers then built working physical prototypes from the files Claude produced. The test checks whether model-generated hardware designs hold up once they leave the screen and get manufactured.

Building strong safety evaluations

Nov 13, 2026 · Engineering

Anthropic shares recommendations for designing, running and reporting AI safety evaluations, drawn from its own testing work. It is aimed at other labs and evaluators who want results that are reliable and comparable.

OpenAI

Control Room: A central system to track and stop model workloads

Nov 9, 2026 · Safety

OpenAI describes Control Room, an internal system that tracks model workloads across its research infrastructure and can quickly halt them if a safety or security concern comes up. It is a concrete example of the operational controls that frontier-lab safety frameworks call for.

Work at the Frontier: AI's effect on freelance work

Nov 10, 2026 · Economics research

In joint research, OpenAI and Upwork find that demand for freelance work is shifting toward new combinations of skills. AI-related projects also come with bigger budgets and more hours. It adds labor-market data to the debate over how AI is changing knowledge work.

Google DeepMind

Gemini Robotics 2.5 accelerates real-world general dexterity

Nov 9, 2026 · Robotics

An upgraded vision-language-action model that turns what a robot sees and is told into physical actions, with better dexterity, reliability and safety than earlier versions. It is DeepMind's latest step toward general-purpose robots that can handle varied real-world manipulation.

Introducing AlphaProof Code: a breakthrough in verifying complex software at depth

Nov 10, 2026 · Formal verification

AlphaProof Code agents wrote roughly three million lines of Lean proofs to formally verify the core encryption used by Signal. It shows AI-driven formal verification working on production-grade security software, not just competition math.

Opening the datasets behind our AI weather models

Nov 10, 2026 · Climate & weather

DeepMind is releasing the foundational precipitation and geostationary-satellite datasets behind its AI weather models. Outside researchers can now train and benchmark against the same observational data.

AlphaGenome 2: Deciphering how cells read DNA to better understand disease

Nov 11, 2026 · Biology

The successor to AlphaGenome predicts how changes in DNA sequence affect specific human cell types and diseases. Researchers can use it to judge which genetic variants are likely to matter, and in which tissues.

Google Research

Exploring the world's languages with Language Explorer

Nov 9, 2026 · NLP / Open data

Google Research introduces Language Explorer, an open tool and dataset resource for browsing information about the world's languages. It supports work on low-resource and under-documented languages. (Synopsis based on the feed title and categories.)

Using wearables to enable cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic (CKM) risk profiling for personalized coaching

Nov 11, 2026 · Health

This work uses data from consumer wearables to estimate a person's combined cardiovascular, kidney and metabolic risk, then uses that profile to personalize health coaching. It builds on last week's wearable hypertension work. (Synopsis based on the feed title and categories.)

Other Labs

A frontier machine intelligence for human neuroimaging

Nov 11, 2026 · Zhao et al. (22 authors)

Neuro-VL is a vision-language model trained on about one million clinical neuroimaging studies. It aims to read and describe brain scans the way a radiologist would, making it one of the largest medical-imaging foundation-model efforts focused on the brain.

Experience-Handoff Reinforcement Learning for Recursive Self-Improvement

Nov 11, 2026 · Kim et al.

Agents are trained with reinforcement learning to write and curate "experience notes" that help later runs of the same or related tasks. Each run passes lessons forward to the next, a step toward agents that improve themselves over time.
