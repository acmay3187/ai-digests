AI Labs Research Digest

Week of Oct 5, 2026 (Oct 3 – Oct 9)

Here's what the major AI labs published this week.

Anthropic

Launching an opt-in vulnerability-finding service for open-source software

Oct 8, 2026 · Frontier Red Team

Anthropic launched OSS Scanner, a free, opt-in service that uses its strongest models, including Claude Mythos, to scan open-source projects for vulnerabilities. Each report comes with a reproducer, an explanation and often a candidate patch, and core maintainers enroll by opening a pull request on Anthropic's GitHub repository. In earlier scanning Anthropic found more than 29,000 candidate vulnerabilities. In a validation sample, 85 of 97 critical and high-severity findings (88%) met its disclosure bar, with only one false positive.

The missing map of the sky

Oct 8, 2026 · Science

Brice Ménard, a Johns Hopkins astrophysicist who is also a researcher at Anthropic, explains how he worked with Claude Science on a project to map the sky. It is the latest in Anthropic's series of case studies on Claude as a research collaborator in the natural sciences.

OpenAI

Sharing AI progress in mathematics

Oct 6, 2026 · Research

OpenAI reports results from an internal model on open mathematics problems, and has published Lean formal proofs and research details on GitHub so others can check them. This comes as researchers debate how far formal verification can certify AI-generated proofs. An arXiv paper the same week (2610.08144) argues that a Lean-verified autoformalization does not guarantee that the original natural-language proof is correct.

Advancing computer use with Ironclad

Oct 6, 2026 · Publication

OpenAI and contract-management company Ironclad are working together to train and evaluate computer-use agents on complex, multi-step contracting workflows. The aim is a realistic, domain-expert testbed for agents that operate software on a user's behalf.

Disrupting AI-enabled "false front" operations

Oct 8, 2026 · Safety

OpenAI's threat-intelligence team describes disrupting two influence operations that used AI to run fake journalists and a fake think tank to push geopolitical messaging. The report documents how the operators used the models and how the accounts were detected.

Our approach to EU text provenance rules

Oct 5, 2026 · Safety

OpenAI sets out how it will watermark generated text to meet EU AI Act provenance requirements. It covers where the watermark applies and how detection works, and explains why access to the detector starts with researchers.

Google DeepMind

EmbeddingGemma 2: an open, lightweight multimodal embedding model

Oct 6, 2026

This is the second generation of DeepMind's small open embedding model, and it now covers more than text. The model maps inputs into vectors for search, retrieval and clustering, and is small enough to run on-device and in RAG pipelines.

Google Research

Open and Emergent Problems in Agentic Privacy and Security: A Contextual Angle

Oct 5, 2026 · Generative AI

Google researchers survey the privacy and security problems that open up once AI agents act for users across tools and data. The post frames them through contextual integrity, the idea that an information flow is appropriate only if it fits the norms of its context, and lists open research problems.

Unlocking Earth AI's planetary geospatial foundation models for global public health

Oct 6, 2026 · Earth AI

Google applies its Earth AI geospatial foundation models, which are trained on planetary-scale satellite and environmental data, to global public-health problems. The post describes how these representations can support health forecasting and planning where local data is sparse.

Does better work always mean better workers?

Oct 7, 2026

This Google Research post asks whether AI assistance that improves the quality of people's output also improves their own skills. It separates gains in work quality from gains in worker capability.

Coverage note: no Meta AI research turned up this week (arXiv had no FAIR-affiliated hits). Product launches such as GPT-6 rolling out in ChatGPT (Oct 7) are left out.
